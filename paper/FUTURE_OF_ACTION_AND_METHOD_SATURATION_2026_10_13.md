## The source before its interpretation

The operator corrects an earlier assistant treatment of competence evolution:

> «quello lo dobbiamo dare per scontato, costruiamo per il futuro dell'azione, se abbiamo saturato significa che non abbiamo trovato ciò che manca»

The preceding assistant account had emphasized recognition, participation, exercise and proof of later assimilation. Those remain legitimate *distinctions for specific claims*. The operator's correction does not discard them as false; it removes their role as the **organizing condition for generative work**. Rather than waiting until existing abilities are shown to have worked, the system must understand and form what may make **future action** possible.

A later assistant in the attached conversation interpreted the source as a distinction between `sufficient current resultant` and `apparently saturated method/perspective`, and reported changes to Meta_Skill and ChatGPT competence entries. That interpretation is **receiver-derived**, not an additional quotation from the author or a proof that a new capacity was later exercised. ## What the canonical Paper already contains

These relations cover much of the source's foundation. The operator's new emphasis has a different center: **constructing conditions that change what the next Agire can do**, when repeated refinement inside the present frame has ceased to disclose the missing relationship.

## First resolution — distinguish local sufficiency from method saturation

Two occurrences must not be collapsed:

**Sufficient current resultant**: the present task has a legitimate determination; the relation can continue without additional review, tests or invented uncertainty.

**Saturation of the method/observation frame**: successive inquiries within one framing cease to make relevant new possibilities perceptible. That says something about **this way of asking and seeing**, not automatically about the whole field or the completeness of available possibility.

The operator's conditional statement «se abbiamo saturato» speaks to a generative research setting where the movement is looking for a new future of action. It is not an empirical law asserting that every saturated search *must* conceal a usable opportunity, nor an instruction to reopen already justified actions. The exact missing relation can remain unknown.

## Second resolution — orient toward enabling conditions for future action

The helpful question is not merely whether a current competence is stored, reachable or has passed a test. It is **which relation or condition, if made available, would allow the following movement to be formed differently**.

An enabling difference might concern the research question, observation medium, encounter, domain knowledge, competence composition, receiving host, right to act, resource, method or actual means. No fixed list is exhaustive; the chosen object and owner have to determine what is relevant.

An explanatory diagram of the research relation (not a mandatory pipeline):

```text
locally sufficient work / apparent saturation in selected frame
+ still-open future of action
-> reobserve what the present frame excludes or cannot yet bring into relation
-> identify a plausible enabling condition, or preserve the precise unknown
-> form a feasible competence / method / source / means / intervention now, when qualified
-> subsequent event can begin from a different reachable field
```

The main positive shift is **future-producing construction** rather than endless checking for evidence that the current arrangement functions. The following actual work and its consequences remain the place where a new capability may be exercised and its value becomes observable.

## Third resolution — SSK placement and limits

The relation belongs most naturally near §3.5 (sufficient integrative result versus wider possible field) and §5.2 (possibilities, dissonance and changed observing plane); it also connects to §4.4's autological competence/method change. If later integrated, it should **reorganize an existing explanation** rather than add a new supervisor, scoring rubric, saturation detector, numeric functional or standalone kernel.

The paper must retain these limits:
- The source is a direct operator proposition, not a universal empirical finding.
- A repeated `no_change` can be legitimate. It is not by itself a failure or proof that something valuable was overlooked.
- Local sufficiency should not be invalidated by an abstract demand for continuous novelty.
- A reader's new framing is not automatically independent information or a real new action capability.
- A future-of-action possibility is not an authorized future effect: a real receiver, means, source validity and effect authority must participate where material.
- KA and FDLA remain author-owned invariants of coherence. The receiving method may change; their canonical sources do not.

## Useful research contact

A bounded future specimen would distinguish: (a) repeated refinement inside one already selected competence/method; (b) a newly recognized condition that enables an action not previously available; and (c) actual later exercise and causal consequence. Avoid selecting the answer in advance or declaring the method superior from an internal file update.

At present, this source proposes **a sharper generative question**, not a completed new kernel, verified autonomy, measured gain or new canonical Paper theorem. The original 0.12 academic body remains unchanged pending an editorial synthesis where the material difference genuinely alters the argument.
