## 1. The relation made inspectable

An AI assistant may be connected to a resource whose relevant content does not enter the context delivered for a particular response. Preservation at the source and delivery through a selected channel are different conditions. The useful question is not only whether the source exists, but which relation can actually participate where the receiving system is doing the work.

SSK already distinguishes the wider field, a receiver's partial perceived context, a knowledge carrier and operational incarnation. This case supplies two concrete documentary instances of that distinction. It does not introduce a new retrieval architecture or identify every response error with a delivery failure.

## 4. Consequence for situated knowledge and action

The source supports a practical distinction among knowledge preserved at origin, knowledge selected and transformed for delivery, content actually received, and its use in a response or action. These are relations within the work, not mandatory stages or additional system components.

A source may genuinely lack the needed distinction. Alternatively, the distinction may exist but be omitted by the selected delivery; it may arrive with an ambiguous referent; or the delivered content may be available but not used appropriately. The corrective operation depends on which relation has changed. A wording instruction alone does not supply a missing source title. Conversely, changing transport alone would not establish that a receiver uses an adequately delivered title correctly.

For SSK, this makes **proactive semantic positioning** concrete at the receiving boundary. It is not enough for a competence to have an address in a catalogue. Knowledge becomes consequential when the pertinent relation can participate through the recipient's actual means. The source owner retains its meaning and authority; a delivered representation does not acquire authority over the source merely by being easier to read.

The same distinction informs the [K-SSK reentry follow-up](SSK_BOUNDED_SELF_GOVERNANCE_CASE_2026_10_16.md#9-documented-follow-up--project-reentry-on-20-october-2026): a revised project entry can make reasons more reachable while leaving their delivery to a scheduled receiver and their later use to be observed separately. The two cases share an analytical relation, not an independent replication or a common measured outcome.

## 5. Supported contribution and remaining unknown

The supported contribution is a source-qualified account of two reported gaps between stored resource knowledge and content supplied to an assistant, together with a reasoned distinction between their possible corrections. No rate of error, latency reduction, superiority of MAIOS, human-comprehension outcome or general K-SSK capability is inferred.
