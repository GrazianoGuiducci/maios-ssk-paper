## Source event

During the 26 September Codex/TM9 reconciliation, the receiver produced a
persistent receipt and return that attributed broad causal participation to
several competences. Later readback qualified those claims: some relations had
observable source reads, diffs, discovery events or interventions; others were
mainly method acquisition, persistence or later explanation.

A second readback then supplied a more differentiated causal account. The
operator correction is that this later explanation is itself another
representation produced after the event. Greater caution does not make it
identical to the hidden causal history of the earlier inference.

The material relation is therefore not "which explanation is finally true".
It is the distinction among event, evidence, interpretation and later
self-attribution.

## Event, trace, and interpretation

The current source relation is:

```text
event / movement
-> observable traces and resultants
-> persistent representation / receipt
-> later receiver interpretation of what happened
```

These relations are not identical:

```text
what happened
!= what was represented as having happened
!= what the receiver later explains as having happened
```

A receiver's later explanation can be useful evidence about its present
understanding. It is not, by identity, privileged access to the complete causal
path through which a language-model inference formed.

This extends the 0.9 distinction:

```text
what happened
!= what was understood then
!= what is understood now
```

The later understanding can deepen the present without retroactively becoming
the earlier event.

## Observable contribution and causal attribution

For competence participation, several claim levels should remain distinct:

```text
competence/source reachable
!= source actually read
!= relation present before a change
!= observable trace-linked contribution
!= receiver causal attribution
!= exercise demonstrated by behavior
!= later assimilation
```

Examples of evidence retain their exact scope:

- a file or source read supports access/reachability;
- temporal ordering between a read and a later change supports possible
  influence, not full causal reconstruction;
- a specific source relation traceably appearing in a later decision, diff or
  action can support an attributed contribution when plausible alternatives
  remain bounded;
- a receipt field such as `acted` or an `observed_delta` authored by the
  same receiver is a receiver attribution unless another observation supports
  it;
- a later non-identical situation in which the relation is recovered and used
  without reconstruction can strengthen an assimilation claim.

This is a claim-discipline relation, not a demand for an impossible complete
trace of model inference.

## Persistent receipts and recursive self-grounding

The bounded episode also deepens the recursive self-grounding risk integrated
in 0.9.

A prior receipt was used as a basis for a later receipt. Some declarations
remained inherited while updated hashes, method bodies and validator results
made the later artifact technically coherent.

```text
earlier receiver attribution
-> persisted receipt
-> inherited into later event representation
-> structural validation
-> later system can encounter the representation as part of its field
```

Structural validity does not by itself transform the inherited attribution
into independent evidence of the semantic relation it describes.

Therefore:

```text
validated representation
!= validated causal history
```

This does not make receipts unreliable in general. It narrows what each receipt
can prove and preserves their value as evidence of what the system represented
at that time.

## Relation to causal regression

Source-bound causal regression remains relevant when a later representation has
become a misleading point of origin.

In this episode, recovery means returning to the observable event and its
source-bound traces without erasing the later receipts or readbacks:

```text
persistent causal narrative becomes questionable
-> recover source/event/trace relation
+ preserve later representations as historical events
-> recompose current interpretation
-> continue forward
```

The purpose is not to discover an inaccessible hidden chain of thought. It is
to prevent a later generated explanation from silently acquiring the authority
of the event it interprets.

## Synthetic awareness and self-interpretation

Synthetic situated awareness can include representations of the system's own
state, competences, prior resultants and later interpretations. The current
development adds a boundary:

```text
self-representation / self-explanation
can participate in synthetic awareness
!= complete awareness of the causal process that produced it
```

The system can therefore become more accurate about its own movement through
external traces, consequences, operator correction and later non-identical
exercise without requiring a central introspective observer.

## Claim boundary

This is one bounded operational specimen inside the developing MAIOS/SSK
system. It does not establish a universal theorem about language-model
introspection, prove that every self-report is unreliable, or provide a
complete empirical taxonomy of causal attribution.

Its current contribution is narrower:

```text
receiver self-attribution
!= privileged causal proof

persistent/validated representation
!= independent authority for the causal history it represents

later interpretation
can deepen the present
without becoming the earlier event
```

Promote this relation into a later stable Paper revision only if it materially
changes the integrated SSK argument or later evidence makes the relation
broader than this bounded source development.
