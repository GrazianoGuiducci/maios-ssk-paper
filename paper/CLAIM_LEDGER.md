The canonical detailed ledger is maintained in
[Appendix A of stable body 0.4](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_4.md#appendix-a-current-claim-state-ledger)
and is interpreted together with the
[dossier's claim-state section](AI_KERNEL_PAPER_DOSSIER_CURRENT.md#stato-delle-affermazioni).

This index prevents a second ledger from drifting away from the manuscript.
The current claim classes are:

```text
operator_source
conceptual_formalization
represented_architecture
bounded_observation
situated_interpretation
hypothesis
experimental_programme
external_literature_claim
retained_unknown
```

Revision 0.4 additionally uses these classes to keep the semantic-core,
receiver-relative incarnation, multimodal participation, displaced perception,
proactive semantic positioning and `possibilistic density` relations distinct
from empirical or metaphysical claims. Update the manuscript ledger first
whenever a claim changes. Update this index only if the classification contract
or canonical location changes.
