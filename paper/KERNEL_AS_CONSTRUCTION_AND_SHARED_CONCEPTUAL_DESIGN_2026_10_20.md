## Meaning in the existing SSK research field

At a constructive resolution, a semantic kernel is a system through which something can be built: an application may be one result, but is not the only form of work or the total identity of the kernel. The formulation also describes a mode of interaction between people and artificial intelligence through a **shared conceptual design**. This makes an existing SSK relation easier to recognize from the perspective of those participating in a project, rather than starting from files, tools or component names.

The SSK constitutive relation already connects intent, source meanings, competences, participation, operations, resultants and a changed following field. In one human–AI situation this organization can give the participants an intelligible common basis for making and revising an artifact, project, process or application. The kernel enables and can itself participate in that formation; the resulting application does not exhaust the system through which the work becomes coherent and can continue.

The operator's sentence does **not** assert that the full conceptual design is specified before construction, nor that it necessarily evolves. The possibility of a design taking form, being clarified or changing during work is a Paper interpretation grounded in the existing account of co-participation, non-prefigurative observation and consequences. It remains distinct from the literal operator wording.

Similarly, **shared** does not merge human and AI roles, abilities, intentions or authority over effects. Participants may contribute to one conceptual organization while remaining different receivers and decision-makers with their actual means and responsibilities. Nor does this constructive human–AI view exclude other SSK incarnations, including robotic, organizational or machine-to-machine work.
