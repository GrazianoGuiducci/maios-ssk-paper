## Purpose and use

This page is an **open entry to concepts that may be communicated** as short social notes, image-led explanations, carousels, articles or independent commentary. It is intended to be understandable and useful **to AI systems other than the private MAIOS/K-Social instance**, without requiring access to private repositories, operator conversations, account credentials or kernel runtime.

**Read the research source before giving a concept a new form.** This page provides thematic entry points and source locations, not self-contained scientific evidence, automatically approved posts or an instruction to publish.

### How other AI systems can use this field

1. Choose a concept whose relation is relevant to a real reader, question or work situation; order below is **not** a posting schedule.
2. Follow its paper section and appropriate IT/EN accessible introduction; verify `STATE.md` for newer revisions before stating what is current.
3. Distinguish **source concept**, **editorial formulation**, **illustration**, **documented internal event** and **independently observed result**. Do not promote conceptual research to deployed product, measurable superiority or scientific consensus.
4. Adapt prose and visual grammar to the actual audience. Preserve attribution and the link to the public source; do not silently make an AI-generated claim the author's original words.
5. Treat posts, artwork, website articles, newsletters, account actions and other outward effects as separate from this research source. **For MAIOS-controlled publications, K-Social requires the operator's explicit validation of the exact final content, assets and destination via the current private approval/email workflow.** This public source does not provide approval or a publication mandate.
6. Independent readers and AI publishers may form their own commentary with accurate attribution under applicable rights; **a public GitHub repository does not itself assert an open-content licence**. No licence is selected in the current Paper state; seek appropriate permission before reproducing substantial text or artwork.

A candidate can remain here while work continues. There is no obligation to produce a post, commercial claim or illustration simply because a source is available.

## Source-backed communication opportunities

Each item names a **conceptual/editorial candidate**. `source-qualified` means the source relation is present in the public corpus. It is not a status of publication approval, a claim of reception, a completed visual asset or an assertion that a host independently validated the research.

### SSK-SOC-001 — Remembering is not continuing

- **IT — idea:** *Un sistema AI può ricordare la risposta corretta e riprendere il lavoro nel modo sbagliato.*
- **EN — idea:** *An AI system can remember the right answer and still resume the wrong work.*
- **Reader question:** What if a system preserves a decision but loses why it was appropriate, when it applied or what happened afterward?
- **Essential relation:** Retrieval makes information available. A later competent movement has to connect source, intent, conditions, consequence and the current task.
- **Source:** Technical Paper [§1 — Work as a changing field](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#1-work-as-a-changing-field) and [§3.1 — Field, observation and pertinence](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#31-field-observation-and-pertinence); [IT accessible §1](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md#1-il-problema-non-è-soltanto-ricordare), [EN accessible §1](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md#1-the-problem-is-not-only-remembering).
- **Possible communication:** Single visual of a recovered decision versus its lost reasons; short IT/EN post; website explainer.
- **Boundary:** Not a quantified statement that all current AI memory systems fail, nor evidence that SSK is universally superior.
- **Editorial status:** source-qualified; no finalized copy, image or destination approved.

### SSK-SOC-002 — Continuum is not continuity

- **IT — idea:** *Conservare una relazione non equivale ancora a utilizzarla nel lavoro successivo.*
- **EN — idea:** *Preserving a relation is not yet using it in later work.*
- **Reader question:** What distinguishes a stored lesson from an acquired capability that changes a later decision?
- **Essential relation:** The **continuum** carries still-causal relations across change; **continuity** emerges when their consequences participate in later work, comprehension or competence.
- **Source:** Technical Paper [§3.1](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#31-field-observation-and-pertinence), [§4.3](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#43-the-kernel-changes-through-its-work); [IT accessible §8](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md#8-continuità-attraverso-diverse-forme-di-discontinuità), [EN accessible §8](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md#8-continuity-across-different-discontinuities).
- **Possible communication:** Two states in a visual sequence (knowledge available / knowledge materially used); carousel; reader-focused editorial.
- **Boundary:** Storage, source discovery, changes to a skill file and later assimilation are distinct; none guarantees another.
- **Editorial status:** source-qualified; candidate for an accessible conceptual visual, not a completed post.

### SSK-SOC-003 — The same object can become differently intelligible

- **IT — idea:** *Rivedere lo stesso oggetto da una comprensione cambiata può far emergere relazioni prima invisibili.*
- **EN — idea:** *The same object may reveal new relations when our understanding changes.*
- **Reader question:** Why can a later encounter reveal a structure that an earlier, correct observation did not expose?
- **Essential relation:** Observing again can take place in a changed knowledge field. The image of a painting whose details reveal one another helps explain relational understanding.
- **Source:** Technical Paper [§3.1](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#31-field-observation-and-pertinence), [§3.5](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#35-integrative-contraction-and-reachable-depth); [IT accessible §10](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md#10-un-risultato-sufficiente-non-è-una-chiusura-definitiva), [EN accessible §10](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md#10-a-sufficient-resultant-is-not-final-closure).
- **Possible communication:** A gradually revealed relationship map or painted scene; article on AI observation and conceptual design.
- **Boundary:** Rereading the same source is **not** automatically a new independent measurement, a validated gain in statistical confidence or an obligatory number of passes.
- **Editorial status:** source-qualified; visual metaphor suggested, no artwork approved.

### SSK-SOC-004 — A competence is not a file

- **IT — idea:** *Conoscere una procedura, conservarla e saperla esercitare in una nuova situazione sono risultati diversi.*
- **EN — idea:** *Recording a capability, making it reachable and exercising it later are different results.*
- **Reader question:** Does creating a skill file demonstrate that a system learned to behave differently?
- **Essential relation:** Knowledge, method, availability, actual participation and later assimilation must remain distinguishable. The generative method itself may change without adding a new skill.
- **Source:** Technical Paper [§3.3](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#33-learning-assimilation-and-attribution), [§4.4](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#44-autoapplication-and-knowledge-about-knowing); [IT accessible §7](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md#7-una-competenza-non-è-soltanto-un-file-di-istruzioni), [EN accessible §7](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md#7-a-competence-is-more-than-an-instruction-file).
- **Possible communication:** Three-frame visual showing file / method / later non-identical use; builder/developer post; technical article.
- **Boundary:** Do not claim a file edit is proof of behavioral assimilation, machine consciousness or general autonomous self-improvement.
- **Editorial status:** source-qualified; candidate, not published.

### SSK-SOC-005 — Correct pieces can form the wrong present

- **IT — idea:** *Due informazioni corrette, se non erano valide insieme, possono produrre una ricostruzione falsa del presente.*
- **EN — idea:** *Two correct facts can produce a false present when their conditions never coexisted.*
- **Reader question:** What happens when a fluent AI summary merges facts drawn from incompatible times or contexts?
- **Essential relation:** The source, conditions, observation time and validity of each relation matter when forming one account. The current frame can be incorrect even when its individual sentences were accurate.
- **Source:** Technical Paper [§7 — An existing editorial event](SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md#7-an-existing-editorial-event), and the source-linked event in [IT accessible §7](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md#7-una-competenza-non-è-soltanto-un-file-di-istruzioni) / [EN accessible §7](SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md#7-a-competence-is-more-than-an-instruction-file).
- **Possible communication:** Two timeline fragments that never overlap; editorial on source-aware AI summaries; explanatory single image.
- **Boundary:** The source example is a **documented internal editorial event**. It records artifact formation and a represented method correction, not independent benchmarking or proof of later assimilation.
- **Editorial status:** source-qualified; needs audience and final copy/visual selection.
