## Variance and self-supporting relations

A sufficiently consolidated relation need not be continuously reconstructed or re-proved when no material difference acts on it.

```text
sufficient resultant R
+ no material new variance
-> R remains capable of carrying its relation
```

This does not make R eternally true or closed. It means that continuation does not require an additional control cycle merely to preserve what the field has already determined.

When new material variance enters:

```text
R
+ material differential
-> dissonance / new relation / changed condition
-> current field may no longer be sufficient
```

The differential can expose a relation that was secondary, latent, unrepresented or not integrable on the prior observation plane.

## Dissonance as differential

Dissonance is not assigned one meaning in advance.

It may be contamination, real difference, source loss, a missing relation, still-unformed potential, or the sign that the current observation plane cannot contain what is becoming present.

The deeper D-ND source contains the operative relations:

- the observation can be contaminated by the form through which it is represented;
- contradiction can be a passage between planes rather than destruction;
- "la dissonanza è il segnale; l'errore è il varco";
- an error can act as a relational counterpoint that moves observation to a different plane.

Therefore:

```text
dissonance
-> preserve the differential long enough for its relation to become visible

if source was lost
-> causal regression

if a new relation becomes visible
-> changed observation / changed pertinence

what was secondary in F_n
may become primary in F_(n+1)
```

No fixed error-processing pipeline follows. The role of the dissonance is determined by the changed field.
