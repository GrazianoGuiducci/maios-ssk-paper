## Contribution to the argument

Understanding can change the relations that become observable, the questions
that can be posed and the methods through which an inquiry proceeds.
Observation can in turn alter that understanding. The observer's participation
and the forming field therefore remain revisable within the coupled event.
A position fixed before the event or an inventory of already represented
relations would supply a narrower model than this reciprocal research relation.

The operating-system direction joins this reciprocity to the organization of
AI work: selecting and forming competences, deciding what knowledge should
continue, and allowing the methods of subsequent work to develop. The SSK
account gives these functions a semantic and causal articulation. The author
names an aim whose realizations can develop through the research; the existing
system's source methods and each observed effect retain their own identity.

Emergent capabilities are consequently an explicit research direction here.
The document does not define a numerical emergence criterion or establish a
general performance result. A source method, an exercise of that method and
its later use in a different situation provide distinct kinds of information.
This distinction preserves the research possibility rather than replacing it
with the closest demonstrated implementation.
