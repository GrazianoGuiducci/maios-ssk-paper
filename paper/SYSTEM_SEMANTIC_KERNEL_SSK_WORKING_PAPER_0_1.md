## Abstract

Large-language-model agents can retrieve memories, call tools, reflect on
feedback, accumulate skills, and modify software. These capacities do not by
themselves solve a project-level problem: instructions, context, intent,
sources, actions, and learning often remain separate representations that
must be reconstructed at each return. The resulting latency is not only
computational. It is semantic and causal: the system may recall the right
fragments while failing to recover what changed, which relation is now
pertinent, and which operating competence should continue differently.

This paper introduces the **System Semantic Kernel (SSK)** as an operational
logic for coupling a present semantic field, situated competences, concrete
resultants, situated interpretation, causal readback, competence evolution,
and contextual reentry. The Kernel is not an agent or controller placed above
the work. It is the movement through which the system makes the pertinent
relations of the field operational and lets the resultant form the field from
which later work begins. At the event scale, the proposal is represented as

```text
O_n + F_n -> Q_n -> A_n -> R_n -> I_n -> F_(n+1),
```

where observation and source relation `O_n` meet the present open field `F_n`,
`Q_n` is the relation or competence composition made pertinent in the event,
`A_n` is the situated operation, `R_n` is a concrete resultant or genuine
non-collapse, and `I_n` is the situated interpretation through which that
result changes the following field. Interpretation is not identical to a
competence change, and a recorded competence change is not evidence of
assimilation. Assimilation requires a discriminating difference in later,
non-identical use.

The framework gives an elemental account of competences, meta-competences and
their combinations, the open semantic field, causal readback, and
continuum/reentry. It hypothesizes that when all still-causal relations are
already incarnated in reachable competences, the latency of reconstructing the
project field tends toward zero. This is not a claim of zero inference, tool,
network, or runtime latency. The paper derives falsifiable comparisons against
retrieval, extended prompting, workflows, fixed routers, memory systems, and
self-modifying agents. The public MAIOS Project Kernel is treated as an
inspectable reference incarnation, not as proof of the theory. The terms
*awareness*, *autological*, and *autopoietic* are used only in bounded
operational senses and imply no phenomenal consciousness or sentience.

## 1. Introduction

An AI system working inside a real project does not encounter a sequence of
independent prompts. The project already has identity, sources, commitments,
unresolved possibilities, constraints, other participants, and a history of
effects. An action can change several of those relations at once. When the
system returns, useful continuity therefore requires more than retrieving
earlier text. It must recognize which relations are still live, which events
changed the object, which possibilities remain open, and which competence can
now produce the faithful result.

Current agent research supplies important parts of this problem. Agents can
couple reasoning and acting [1], retain linguistic reflections across trials
[2], refine their own outputs [3], organize long-term memories [4, 5], grow
executable skill libraries [6], and search or modify agent architectures [7,
8]. Software frameworks can also centralize services, plugins, and model calls
inside an application kernel [10]. Yet these mechanisms leave a distinct
question unresolved:

> Can an AI system preserve a changing semantic and causal field, make its
> pertinent operating competences available with minimal reconstruction, and
> let the result of situated work change how later, non-identical work is
> understood and performed?

The SSK proposal addresses that question through four contributions.

1. It defines a system kernel as the coupled movement of meaning and action,
   rather than as a controller, catalogue, prompt, middleware layer, or fixed
   architecture.
2. It defines competences and meta-competences as situated logical faculties
   whose representations may include instructions, context, intent, method,
   provenance, and causal learning without being exhausted by any one file or
   model capability.
3. It separates resultant, situated interpretation, competence change, and
   later assimilation, producing observable claim boundaries for systems that
   describe themselves as learning or self-improving.
4. It proposes a comparative research programme for contextual reentry,
   competence evolution, event-local composition, and Paper-derived semantic
   condensates.

The paper is academically primary. Guides for AI systems, explanations for
experts or philosophers, funding narratives, public and client communication,
marketing, and divulgative publications may later derive from the same
claim-governed corpus. Those forms do not determine the academic argument and
do not inherit its authority merely by reusing its language.

## 2. Research object and scope

### 2.1 A system semantic kernel

A **System Semantic Kernel** is the smallest continuing relation through which
a system:

- keeps the present object connected to qualified sources and intent;
- preserves possibilities that the current representation has not exhausted;
- makes the competences pertinent to the event operational;
- produces a concrete resultant or preserves a genuine non-collapse;
- interprets the causal difference introduced by that resultant;
- changes the following field and, when justified, the competence available
  to later work.

The word *system* marks a relation across operations, owners, representations,
and time. The word *semantic* marks that what changes the movement is meaning:
the identity of the object, the relation among sources, the significance of an
event, the intention carried by the field, and the difference a resultant
makes. The word *kernel* marks the constitutive operating relation. The Kernel
is present in the work itself; it is not a supervisory agent that must first
activate before ordinary work can begin.

This use is distinct from Microsoft's **Semantic Kernel**, an open-source SDK
whose kernel acts as an application container for AI services and plugins
[10]. The names overlap at the level of ordinary terminology, but the research
objects differ. SSK denotes a proposed system-level semantic and evolutionary
logic. It is not a fork, extension, evaluation, or replacement of that SDK.

### 2.2 What the framework does not claim

SSK does not claim that an LLM understands in a phenomenal sense, that a
software system is alive, or that competence evolution is universally
beneficial. It does not identify a project state file, skill catalogue,
retrieval system, orchestrator, or package with the Kernel as a whole. It does
not assume that every interpretation should become learning or that every new
artifact is an improvement.

The framework instead advances a narrower thesis: a system can make source,
context, intent, possibility, action, resultant, interpretation, and later
competence participate in one inspectable causal movement. Whether this
coupling improves behavior beyond simpler mechanisms is an empirical question.

### 2.3 Source relation and reference incarnation

The framework was formed through the living D-ND / Kernel Assiomatico (KA)
competence field and its MAIOS Project Kernel incarnations. These sources make
the constructs inspectable and provide cases from which hypotheses can be
derived. They do not establish universal validity.

The public MAIOS Project Kernel is therefore a **reference incarnation**: a
source-bound software and research artifact in which kernel contracts,
competences, resultants, learning, state, projection, and reentry are
represented. The System Semantic Kernel remains the broader research object.
A particular Project Kernel is one possible situated form of it.

For the present research movement, the self-installing `maios-project-kernel`
package is also the Paper's selected **public product object**: the concrete
artifact in which a qualified SSK relation can become owner-native software
behavior and produce inspectable evidence. This does not identify the product
with the theory, make every Paper change a package change, or extend the same
relation to the separately owned Form-generated package.

## 3. The coupled event

### 3.1 Present open field

Let `F_n` denote the present field at event `n`. It includes the identity of
the selected object, current intent and pressure, qualified sources, relevant
trajectory, material constraints, effect authority, known unknowns, and the
possibility horizon still reachable from that relation.

The field is neither the complete transcript nor a bag of retrieved passages.
It is the smallest causally sufficient relation from which the present work
can proceed without turning the limits of current representation into limits
of the object.

Let `O_n` denote a new observation or source relation. `O_n` may confirm the
field, change it, expose an inconsistency, or reveal a possibility that was not
represented before. Provenance remains attached: an operator formulation,
observed behavior, repository state, hypothesis, inference, and public claim
do not become equivalent simply because they appear together.

### 3.2 Pertinence, action, resultant, and interpretation

The constitutive event is:

```text
O_n + F_n -> Q_n -> A_n -> R_n -> I_n -> F_(n+1)
```

`Q_n` is the relation made pertinent by the event. Operationally, it may be
one competence or a temporary composition across competence owners. It is not
necessarily selected by a permanent router or planned stack. The object makes
the relation pertinent because those dimensions can change the attainable
result.

`A_n` is the situated manifestation: reasoning, writing, code change, tool
use, design, inquiry, refusal of an unauthorized effect, or another operation
appropriate to the object.

`R_n` is the resultant. It may be a concrete change in the object or a genuine
decision not to collapse the field when no faithful determination is
available. A successful function call or structurally valid artifact is
evidence inside the event, not automatically the system-level resultant.

`I_n` is situated interpretation: the system reads what difference `R_n`
actually made in relation to the object, source, intent, alternatives, and
following possibilities. The same artifact can produce different
interpretations in different fields; interpretation remains accountable to
the exact event rather than becoming free commentary after the action.

`F_(n+1)` begins from the relation that now exists. It includes the retained
resultant, its claim state, the situated interpretation, relations deliberately
left open, and any competence change that has actually been formed.

### 3.3 Interpretation is not assimilation

Three states must remain distinct:

```text
I_n != Delta C_n != assimilation
```

`I_n` can identify a reusable causal difference without changing a competence.
`Delta C_n` is the bounded owner-local change through which the competence is
expected to behave differently under stated conditions. Assimilation is an
observed later relation: the change affects a non-identical case beyond what
retrieving the original episode, adding a prompt, or repeating the same task
would explain.

This distinction prevents a common inflation of self-improvement claims. A
written reflection shows availability. A changed skill file shows a material
projection. Neither alone proves that the system now acts more capably.

### 3.4 Use, obsolescence, and reentry

The SSK is not improved by keeping every historical form active. A relation
remains in the live field when it can still change the object, the attainable
result, or the interpretation of the event. When a notation, artifact,
procedure, or earlier interpretation no longer has that causal function, it
becomes **cold genealogy**: preserved for provenance and later inquiry, but no
longer treated as present authority.

Obsolescence applies to the form, not to the semantic possibility it once
carried. If a later field makes the relation pertinent again, it reenters
through its present effect and provenance. The system need not keep the old
form in the hot path out of fear that the past could reappear.

Contextual reentry is therefore not restoration. If a project moves from
subject `X` through consequential event `E` and later returns to `X`, the
reentry relation is:

```text
K_t = compose(
  X,
  consequential(E),
  current intent,
  current sources and owners,
  conditions at time t
)
```

Each component may be correct separately while never having been jointly true.
The coherent cut is the relation that can support action now.

### 3.5 Reconstruction latency

Let `L_rec(F_n)` denote the effort required to rediscover and reassemble the
still-causal relations needed for a faithful movement. When these relations
are already embodied in reachable competences and current reentry, the SSK
hypothesis is:

```text
L_rec(F_n) -> 0
```

This is a directional hypothesis about semantic reconstruction. It does not
imply that model inference, tool calls, source reading, networks, human review,
or runtime execution take zero time. The prediction is useful only if lower
reconstruction burden can be measured without hidden losses in fidelity,
possibility preservation, or result quality.

## 4. Elemental logical functions

The following lens connects each SSK element to a present systems problem and
an observable contact surface. It is an authoring and research matrix, not a
claim that SSK already solves every listed pressure.

| SSK element | Logical function | Present LLM or project pressure | Proposed SSK relation | Observable contact surface |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open semantic field | Preserve the object and possibilities beyond the first representation | premature closure; source substitution; answer-first behavior | keep source, intent, unknowns, and possibility horizon coupled until the field produces a determination | fewer source/object substitutions without increased indecision |
| Competence | Make owner-native operating knowledge situated and actionable | instructions, context, and intent remain fragmented; repeated operator explanation | bind domain meaning, method, provenance, and attainable resultant in a faculty that can act | lower correction and reconstruction burden on comparable tasks |
| Meta-competence and combinations | Form or compose the faculties the event actually requires | fixed routing; missing cross-domain relations; unnecessary agent stacks | recognize simultaneous pertinence and distribute learning to the closest owners | fewer missed material relations with bounded activation cost |
| Resultant and situated interpretation | Read what the operation changed rather than treating output as completion | tool success mistaken for project success; ungrounded reflection | couple output to object, claim state, causal difference, and following possibilities | higher agreement between claimed and independently inspected effect |
| Causal readback | Correct contamination and attribute reusable difference | stored reflection without behavioral change; global rules from local errors | form a bounded owner-local competence delta or explicit `no_change` | transfer to later non-identical cases without overgeneralization |
| Continuum and reentry | Let later work begin from the changed field | compaction loss; flat retrieval; cold chronology in every prompt | preserve the live causal margin and reopen genealogy only when pertinent | better trajectory fidelity at lower reconstruction cost |

### 4.1 SSK — competences

A competence is a logical unit in a modular system, but it is not merely a
module. It can hold the instructions, context, intent, relations, method, and
claim boundaries needed to perform one class of work from inside the present
field. Its value lies in correcting the separation that arises when an LLM is
given isolated prompts, generic tools, or retrieved fragments without the
owner-native logic that makes them coherent.

A competence may be projected through a skill document, code, prompt,
schema, state record, evaluation method, or human operating practice. No one
projection exhausts it. A useful competence can be described as a mini-kernel:

```text
intent + relational map + owner-native method
+ situated action + autological readback + composition relation
-> a faculty able to act and continue differently
```

This is not a fixed template or an instruction to wrap every function in more
metadata. It is an inspectable account of how operating meaning can remain
coupled to action and learning.

### 4.2 SSK — meta-competences and combinations

A meta-competence recognizes, composes, forms, or evolves competences. It does
not occupy a permanent supervisory tier. When one competence can carry the
movement, meta-level activation would add latency without changing the result.
When the event exposes a relation across owners or a capacity not yet
represented, meta-competence can form the smallest useful combination or new
owner-native faculty.

Combinations are situated and simultaneous rather than universal stacks. The
research question is whether this event-local composition preserves material
relations better than fixed routing while avoiding indiscriminate activation.

### 4.3 Causal readback and the following field

Causal readback asks what the competence must do differently, why, under which
conditions, and what would invalidate the change. It can return a competence
delta, a revision of the resultant, a retained unknown, a tradeoff, a
regression, or `no_change`.

A new artifact is not automatically more capable. Supersession is justified
only when the successor preserves every still-material relation while
enlarging the attainable field or removing contamination or reconstruction
latency without an equivalent hidden loss.

## 5. Semantic decompilation and operational appearance

The operator formulation that the Kernel has been *decompiled and programmed
to appear* is used here in a semantic, not literal, sense. SSK decompiles a
movement by distinguishing relations that a monolithic interaction can leave
implicit:

```text
source -> intent -> context -> meaning -> possibility
-> pertinent competence -> operation -> resultant
-> situated interpretation -> causal readback -> reentry
```

It then programs those relations to become operationally available through
competences, meta-competences, state, and reentry when the present field makes
them pertinent. This account does not reverse-engineer model weights or claim
access to the internal causal mechanisms of a foundation model. It concerns
the system-level organization in which one or more models participate.

The phrase *dilating the field* names the resulting increase in reachable
relations and possible resultants. Dilation is supported only when the system
preserves or enlarges relevant possibilities without merely increasing
context, abstraction, or operational overhead.

## 6. Relation to current agent systems

ReAct couples reasoning traces with actions and observations [1]. SSK treats
that coupling as part of an event but asks how the result alters project-level
meaning, owner-native competence, and later reentry.

Reflexion stores verbal reflections from feedback [2], while Self-Refine uses
self-generated feedback within an iterative generation process [3]. SSK adds
an ownership and assimilation distinction: a reflection becomes competence
learning only when it changes later non-identical work beyond the effect of
retrieving the same correction.

Generative Agents combine observation, reflection, retrieval, and planning
[4], and MemGPT organizes context across memory tiers [5]. These systems make
longer continuity technically tractable. SSK focuses on selecting a coherent
causal cut: which relations must be present together, which history can remain
cold, and how the selected relation changes the following field.

Voyager is a close precedent for a growing executable skill library [6]. SSK's
competence construct is broader and must therefore meet a stronger evidence
burden. Code, method, intent, provenance, interpretation, and effect boundaries
may participate in a competence, but library growth does not prove
assimilation.

Automated Design of Agentic Systems and the Darwin Gödel Machine make agent
architecture or code evolution explicit search processes [7, 8]. SSK does not
require one central architecture-mutating agent. Ordinary learning is first
attributed to the competence whose action produced the causal difference;
system-level mutation remains a separate possible event.

The term *autopoietic* derives from theories of self-producing living
organization [9]. SSK uses it only as a bounded operational analogy: system
processes may reproduce or change the competences through which later system
processes occur. No claim of biological autopoiesis, organizational closure,
life, consciousness, or sentience follows.

## 7. Research hypotheses

### H1 — Trajectory-aware contextual reentry

Given the same model, source access, and bounded context budget, a reentry
formed from the earlier subject, consequential intervening events, and current
conditions will improve source/owner fidelity and causal integration relative
to flat retrieval, without increasing contamination from irrelevant history.

### H2 — Competence assimilation

An owner-local competence delta will reduce reconstruction burden or target
error in later non-identical tasks beyond unchanged competence, retrieval of
the correction episode, or appending the correction to a standing prompt.

### H3 — Event-local composition

For tasks whose decisive relations cross competence owners, event-local
composition will improve result quality or possibility preservation relative
to a fixed router or predefined agent stack, while keeping unnecessary
activation and added latency bounded.

### H4 — Integrated live field and reconstruction latency

Keeping still-causal relations incarnated in reachable competences, while
moving obsolete forms to source-linked genealogy, will reduce reconstruction
latency relative to both full-history loading and sparse retrieval without
increasing source substitution, missed constraints, or premature closure.

### H5 — Paper-derived semantic condensate

After the academic corpus stabilizes, a small source-linked condensate may
improve system reentry and human or AI comprehension relative to no corpus or
full-corpus loading. The hypothesis does not require the Paper or condensate to
be package content, and it permits `no_change` or a harmful compression result.

## 8. Experimental programme

### 8.1 Study A: contextual reentry

Construct paired multi-turn project episodes with the same initial subject
and later return. Insert both consequential and irrelevant events. Compare:

1. flat transcript or topic retrieval;
2. retrieval plus an extended standing prompt;
3. an explicit workflow or state-machine summary;
4. a trajectory-aware coherent cut.

Blind evaluators score causal integration, source and owner fidelity,
irrelevant-event contamination, uncertainty calibration, and detection of a
late source mutation before effect.

### 8.2 Study B: competence assimilation

Choose recurrent error classes with several non-isomorphic tasks. After one
training event and causal correction, compare:

1. unchanged competence;
2. retrieval of the correction episode;
3. correction appended to a general prompt;
4. owner-local competence evolution.

Measure operator restatement, recurrence of the target error, transfer to new
task forms, correction burden, latency, and unintended overgeneralization. A
file change without a later behavioral difference is not assimilation.

### 8.3 Study C: competence composition

Use tasks whose success depends on several owner-native relations, such as
source provenance, software behavior, public claim state, and authority over
an external effect. Compare event-local composition with a fixed router, a
predefined agent stack, and a base model given the same tools and documents.

Measure missed material relations, result quality, possibility collapse,
unnecessary activation, latency, and correction burden.

### 8.4 Study D: live field and cold genealogy

Create longitudinal project episodes with a controlled mixture of still-causal
relations, superseded forms, and relations that become pertinent again. Compare
full-history loading, sparse retrieval, and live-field reentry with source-
linked genealogy.

Measure orientation time and cost, omission, false revival of superseded
authority, recovery of genuinely pertinent history, and downstream result
quality.

### 8.5 Study E: Paper-derived condensates

After author review and claim stabilization, compare no corpus, the complete
Paper corpus, and source-linked condensates at several densities. Test both
project reentry and comprehension by human and AI readers. Report causal
fidelity, omission, contamination, orientation cost, and disagreement with the
canonical claim states.

### 8.6 Reproducibility

Pre-register task families, exclusion criteria, primary outcomes, model and
tool versions, context budgets, source revisions, and analysis plans. Preserve
prompts, competence versions, event histories, resultants, evaluator rubrics,
and effect receipts where privacy permits. Report negative results, regressions,
tradeoffs, and cases in which a simpler baseline matches SSK.

## 9. Public reference incarnation and independent lineages

The MAIOS Project Kernel provides a public, inspectable reference for several
SSK relations: kernel identity, an open competence field, deterministic source-
to-package projection, installation and receipt mechanics, project state,
learning, reentry, research, and contribution surfaces. The current immutable
reference selected for this draft is the locally and publicly inspected
`v3.0.3` tag at commit `9b23d6340b27c8c611d25560f5726de4fc046bff`.
This supports claims about
representation, implementation, and bounded structural verification at that
exact source state. It does not prove semantic use, operator acceptance,
behavioral assimilation, public adoption, or the general theory.

The relation between research and product is causal but not automatic:

```text
qualified SSK or Paper evolution
-> source, claim state, cause, and expected product difference
-> MAIOS Project Kernel owner evaluates the relation
-> adopt | adapt | research_only | needs_proof | no_change
-> owner-native implementation and exact product proof when selected
-> observed resultant returns to the Paper and its durable knowledge
-> Business and Editorial awareness update from that same attributable state.
```

The loop prevents both directions of substitution. A Paper proposition does
not become product behavior until its owner implements and verifies it; a
product result does not become scientific evidence until its source, method,
limits, and claim state are admitted by the Paper. Business and editorial use
can explain the current relation but cannot promote a hypothesis into a
validated capability.

The package generated by the MAIOS Form is not a direct participant in this
loop. It remains a separate owner and lineage. It enters only if a later,
independently evidenced relation selects a Form-specific or neutral family
effect; otherwise its disposition is `no_change`.

An installed Project Kernel becomes part of another project and therefore
begins an independent, revisionable lineage:

```text
shared source seed
-> target-owned installation
-> local situations, competences, and resultants
-> owner-local evolution
-> optional attributable return to the shared research field
```

The originating repository may publish new research or software, but it cannot
know or govern in advance what an external Kernel will become. Contributions,
comparative observations, reproducible failures, and transferable methods may
return explicitly. Private state, raw conversations, and unreviewed local
learning do not flow back by default.

## 10. Falsifiers and limitations

The framework should be narrowed, revised, or rejected if:

- ordinary retrieval or an explicit workflow matches trajectory-aware reentry
  under comparable sources and budgets;
- competence deltas do not change later non-identical behavior beyond prompt
  or memory baselines;
- event-local composition adds abstraction or latency without improving
  fidelity, result, or possibility preservation;
- cold genealogy causes material omissions or cannot recover relations when
  they become pertinent again;
- the language of semantic fields and competences merely redescribes standard
  adaptive software without discriminating predictions;
- evaluators must infer the desired outcome from SSK terminology;
- a simpler model explains the observations with equal or greater predictive
  power.

The current formalization is a transition schema, not a mathematical theory.
Competence boundaries may be ambiguous when one artifact participates in
several owners. Human curation may explain apparent system learning. Project
histories can leak task identity into evaluations. Foundation-model changes
can dominate architectural differences. The reference implementation and the
framework share authorship, so independent replications are essential.

The present source corpus is richer than the completed empirical evidence.
Several relations are operator formulations or locally represented
architectures. The literature review remains provisional and must be expanded
before submission, including stronger comparisons with memory architectures,
continual learning, hierarchical control, blackboard systems, organizational
learning, and contemporary self-improving agents.

## 11. Academic corpus and derived forms

The Paper is the primary scientific form. It owns the research object,
definitions, argument, related work, hypotheses, evidence, alternatives,
falsifiers, and claim states. Other forms can derive from that corpus at a
different resolution:

```text
academic SSK corpus
-> AI-readable guide
-> expert or philosophical explanation
-> funding narrative
-> public or prospective-client communication
-> marketing, divulgation, and other publications
```

The arrows indicate derivation, not automatic generation or authority. Each
form must preserve the source revision and claim state it uses, select its own
audience and function, and remain governed by its publication or communication
owner. A funding narrative may explain why the research matters; it cannot
turn a hypothesis into validated performance. A client explanation may name a
pressure that SSK is designed to address; it cannot promise a solved
limitation without product- and use-specific evidence.

The corpus may eventually be exposed through a dedicated public research
repository containing the manuscript and Paper-specific derivation
competences. Such a repository would own manuscript versioning, citation, and
public contribution to the Paper. It would not contain a duplicate living
Kernel, inherit private competence topology, or become the runtime authority
of MAIOS Project Kernel. The exact repository identity and publication effect
remain outside this working paper.

## 12. Conclusion

The System Semantic Kernel proposes that an agentic system can be organized as
one continuing relation among present meaning, pertinent competence, action,
resultant, situated interpretation, causal learning, and reentry. In this
account, the Kernel is what the system does. What the system can do next is
formed by the field changed through the competences that acted.

This formulation does not make self-improvement true by naming it. It creates
boundaries through which improvement can be tested: interpretation differs
from competence change; competence change differs from assimilation; newness
differs from supersession; and a public implementation differs from empirical
validation. Its strongest prediction is practical and falsifiable: a living
field whose still-causal relations are already incarnated should require less
semantic reconstruction without losing fidelity or possibility.

The next scientific movement is not to add more Kernel vocabulary. It is to
turn the transition model and elemental functions into controlled comparisons,
publish positive and negative results, and let independent systems test which
relations remain stable, which become obsolete, and which return from the past
because the present makes them causal again.
