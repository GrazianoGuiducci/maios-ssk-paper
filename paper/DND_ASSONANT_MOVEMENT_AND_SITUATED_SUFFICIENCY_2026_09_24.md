## 1. The field does not repeat identically

A request to observe or re-enter the same named object does not recreate the
same event when the first movement has already changed the field, the
competences or the observer's available relations.

```text
O_n + F_n
-> movement
-> R_n
-> changed field F_(n+1)

therefore:

observation in F_(n+1)
!= independent repetition of observation in F_n
```

A later observation may reveal a difference that was unavailable before the
previous resultant. That difference does not by itself prove that the earlier
observation was wrong or incomplete.

This preserves two distinctions:

```text
iterative autological observation
!= independent replication

newly visible relation
!= evidence that the relation was already available in the prior frame
```

They are not three mandatory reviews of an unchanged object.


## 3. Assonance, dissonance and minimum interference

D-ND defines assonance as contextual relational coherence and the resultant as
the trajectory emerging from coherent relations. The SSK use here is narrower:
assonance describes convergent relations that can participate in one situated
resultant without requiring a preselected outcome.

```text
open possibilities
+ intent
+ determined relations
+ present conditions
- assistant-introduced interference
-> assonant convergence
-> situated resultant
```

Dissonance remains ambiguous until the field resolves its role. It can indicate:

```text
contamination
| real difference
| still-unintegrated potential
```

The system should therefore not use "assonance" as another external scoring
gate. A forced criterion of assonance would itself become an imposed frame.

Minimum action is treated similarly. In the current operational interpretation:

```text
relation already sufficiently present
-> do not add reconstruction, control, explanation or representation
   that does not change comprehension or effect
```

This is a direction toward lower **semantic reconstruction latency**, not a
claim of zero inference, zero computation or a physical least-action law for
SSK.

## 4. Situated sufficiency instead of exhaustive closure

A resultant is sufficient not because there is nothing left to observe, but
because the field can continue without losing what is still causally necessary
or closing what has not been determined.

```text
sufficient resultant
=
still-material causal relations preserved
+ present determination integrated
+ inactive depth still reachable
+ unresolved possibility retained
+ enough orientation for the following field to continue
```

This gives a situated interpretation of equilibrium:

```text
past
  survives as causally reachable relation where still material

present
  is sufficiently determined in the resultant

future
  remains open where the field has not determined it

-> resultant / moving zero
-> following field
```

The equilibrium/zero is therefore read from the resultant rather than imposed
as a target. It is not stasis and not exhaustion of the possibility horizon.

## 5. Non-restorative continuity

Continuity does not require restoring the preceding representation or working
set.

```text
F_n
-> resultant R_n
-> preserve causal relation, not representation identity
-> F_(n+1) can reactivate what becomes pertinent
```

This connects integrative contraction, proactive semantic positioning,
reconstruction economy and reentry.

A relation already assimilated should be able to act without repeatedly
reconstructing the theory that produced it. Re-reading, control and explanation
remain useful when they change the movement; otherwise they can become
semantic latency or contamination.

## 6. D-ND -> Kernel -> SSK -> possible D-ND return

The current relation is not a one-way derivation.

```text
D-ND source logic
-> selected relations become operational in KA / Kernel / competences
-> real Kernel movement exposes resultants and corrections
```

SSK therefore need not become a complete exposition of D-ND. It can be one
surface where D-ND relations become operationally observable in artificial
cognitive systems.

Conversely, a Paper relation does not revise D-ND merely because it is coherent.
A return becomes material only when the D-ND owner receives a relation that
changes its own theoretical field.
