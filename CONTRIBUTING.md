# Contributing to the SSK Paper

The most useful contributions make one source-bound difference inspectable.
They may identify a stronger neighbouring theory, missing primary literature,
an ambiguous construct, a counterexample, a falsifier, a reproducible event,
or a clearer way to preserve an existing claim without changing its state.

## Before contributing

1. Read the [current state](STATE.md) and the relevant manuscript section.
2. Identify the exact statement, construct, source, or protocol affected.
3. Classify the contribution as one of:
   `source`, `critique`, `alternative`, `counterexample`, `falsifier`,
   `observation`, `protocol`, `editorial clarification`, or `derivative`.
4. State what the contribution changes and what remains unknown.
5. Link primary sources when the contribution relies on external research.

Repository issues are the preferred entry for critique and research proposals.
A pull request should preserve provenance and must not silently promote a
hypothesis, implementation, or local observation to a general result.

## Current rights boundary

The repository does not yet declare a reuse license or final contribution
terms. Until that decision is recorded, use issues for proposals that would
otherwise require importing substantial third-party or contributor-owned text.
Do not submit confidential material, private conversations, credentials,
personal data, or source material you are not entitled to share.

## Review states

A contribution can be discussed, represented, accepted into a working
revision, author-reviewed, peer-reviewed, or published. These are distinct
states. Acceptance into the repository does not imply scientific validation,
product adoption, package inclusion, website publication, or runtime use.
