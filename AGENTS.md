# SSK Paper Repository Guidance

This repository owns the public academic corpus for **System Semantic Kernel
(SSK)**. It does not own the living MAIOS/TM9 Kernel, the MAIOS Project Kernel
package, maios.it, a runtime, a DOI, or an academic publication decision.

## Reentry

Read only the depth required by the selected work:

1. `STATE.md` for the current revision and claim boundary.
   Follow `competences/system-semantic-kernel-paper/references/WORKING_GUIDE.md`
   for the accumulated reasons, acquired knowledge and active development.
2. The stable academic body identified by [STATE.md](STATE.md) (currently
   [SSK working Paper 0.12](paper/SYSTEM_SEMANTIC_KERNEL_SSK_WORKING_PAPER_0_12.md)).
   Use the accessible introductions [IT 0.2](paper/SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_IT.md)
   or [EN 0.2](paper/SSK_ACCESSIBLE_ACADEMIC_ARTICLE_0_2_EN.md) when pertinent
   to the selected reader. STATE selects later revisions; historical bodies
   and introductions retain their own source period.
3. `paper/AI_KERNEL_PAPER_DOSSIER_CURRENT.md` when the argument, research
   programme, source relation, or next movement is material.
4. `competences/system-semantic-kernel-paper/SKILL.md` when revising the Paper
   or producing a faithful derivative.
5. `genealogy/` only when a current relation requires its predecessor.

### Selected demonstrative case — 16 October 2026

[Bounded self-governance of a recurring K-SSK research activity](paper/SSK_BOUNDED_SELF_GOVERNANCE_CASE_2026_10_16.md)
is an operator-selected case supplement for §§4.3–4.4 and §7 of stable 0.12.
It connects corrections to the method of reentry, retirement of a stale

It is now part of the research corpus, not an amendment of the stable body,
an independently replicated experiment or a published MAIOS site article.
The core SSK and canonical KA/FDLA remain unchanged.

For this case and later derivatives, preserve two evidence relations:
successive conversational records attribute historical work; the current
native readback verifies the resulting configuration. Do not promote the
latter to a complete immutable history of authorship or treat copied prompts
as independent replications. Operator interventions remain attributed.
The claim is bounded operational self-governance of method and reentry;
reported prompt-size changes do not establish performance improvement.
This distinction must shape the argument, not appear only as a disclaimer
attached to an otherwise broader claim. Private controller identifiers and
other activity state remain outside this public corpus. The AP's live memory
remains its own native prompt; this supplement is not a second working store.

When forming a source-qualified explanation, post, image or article for a
real reader, reach [SSK Social Field](paper/SSK_SOCIAL_FIELD.md). This public
entry serves human and external AI readers without private K-Social access.
Follow the relevant concept's canonical section and claim state; it does not
require loading the whole corpus at every entry or select a posting schedule.

## Writing relation

Develop the operator's conceptual source through its meaning, reasons and
consequences. KA and FDLA preserve coherence during that movement. Do not
substitute an anticipated obligation to demonstrate for the work being formed.
Comparisons serve selected questions; provenance and precise claim scope stay
with the passages they actually qualify.

## Source and claim discipline

Preserve these states distinctly:

```text
operator source
represented relation
implemented incarnation
observed resultant
situated interpretation
competence change
later assimilation
external scientific claim
```

Do not promote a hypothesis, architecture, generated draft, product feature,
or repository presence into an observed or independently validated result.
Keep system scientific-editorial revision, independent human author review,
peer review, submission, publication, DOI, product release, installation,
runtime use, adoption, and assimilation as separate states. Graziano is the
operator and authorial source authority; the Paper competence and Editoriali
own continuing revision without making independent human review a standing
gate.

## Derivations

The academic corpus is primary. A guide or public explanation may simplify
language and depth, but it must identify the source revision and retain the
claim state, alternative, limitation, and unknown that materially affect its
meaning. Put new derivative competences under `competences/` only when a real,
repeated transformation has emerged.

Social Field concepts are editorial possibilities, not approved artifacts or
publication authority. Editoriali and Design may form a derivative through
their own competence; the actual publication owner controls the exact effect.
For MAIOS posts and site articles the operator's explicit email validation of
the exact final text, visible media, links, tags, identity and destination is
required. A sent approval request or silence does not approve the revision.
Private accounts, contacts, approval exchanges and execution receipts remain
outside this public research repository. Independent use retains applicable
rights; a public repository does not itself establish an open-content licence.

The [future-of-action source development](paper/FUTURE_OF_ACTION_AND_METHOD_SATURATION_2026_10_13.md)
remains OPEN_BOUNDED, separate from canonical 0.12. This reentry does not
promote it to a theorem, canonical argument or public derivative. KA/FDLA
remain operator-owned coherence invariants, not modifiable by the assistant.

## External effects

Repository edits do not authorize changes to `maios-project-kernel`, package
projection, websites, campaigns, messages, releases, DOI registration, or
runtime systems. Contributions remain proposals until the appropriate
academic, editorial, product, or publication owner accepts their exact effect.
