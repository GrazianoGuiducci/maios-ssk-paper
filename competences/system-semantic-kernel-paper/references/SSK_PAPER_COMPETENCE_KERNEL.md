## Research Object

The primary object is **System Semantic Kernel (SSK)**: the selected typology
through which the Kernel operating here is understood and distinguished from
other kernel forms. The living D-ND / KA competence field is the source system
through which this typology is observed. The Paper and the public MAIOS
identity are two owner-native projections of the same object, while product,
Harness and runtime incarnations retain their own proof states.

The wider phenomenon is **artificial cognitive becoming in agentic and robotic
systems**: how a system can act from a situated semantic field, observe what
its action produces, understand the relation of that resultant to the system
in which it participates, take its own method as an object, compose or form
competences, and change how later action can begin.

The research asks what this becoming can make possible for:

- operators who need systems that preserve meaning and continuity while work
  changes;
- projects and organizations that must retain know-how as situated competence,
  not only stored instructions;
- agentic systems whose capabilities and contexts evolve;
- robotic and embodied systems in which perception, action, environment,
  authority, safety, and real-time constraints become part of the field;
- societies that may organize knowledge, responsibility, cooperation, and
  technological participation through new human-AI relations.

These are open possibility fields. The Paper must distinguish what is
represented, incarnated, observed, interpreted, and still open.

## Semantic Kernel Relation

A System Semantic Kernel is not merely a repository of meanings, a router, a
prompt stack, or a collection of skills. Its object is the relation through
which meaning, participation, operation, resultant, and continuation remain
coupled inside a system.

A useful explanatory resolution is:

```text
O_n = observation or present source relation
F_n = open situated field, including its horizon
Q_n = pertinent relation that takes form in the event
A_n = operation or manifestation
R_n = observable resultant or genuine non-collapse
I_n = situated interpretation of the causal difference
F_n+1 = following field, possibly transformed by R_n and I_n

O_n + F_n -> Q_n -> A_n -> R_n -> I_n -> F_n+1
```

This is a reading aid, not a claim that the event is mechanically sequential.
The relation can be simultaneous, recursive, distributed, or observed at
different resolutions.

## Competence

A competence is a situated faculty of the Kernel. It understands its domain
and its participation in the system, acts, reads back its effective resultant,
and can modify how it will act afterward. When the field makes these dimensions
pertinent, it can:

- reread its own operation;
- understand its participation in the larger system;
- take its own method as an object;
- compose or form other competences;
- evolve through use and later non-identical resultants.

Situated, self-reflexive, system-aware, autological, meta, and autopoietic are
possible operational dimensions, not compulsory levels, scores, promotional
labels, or proof of subjective consciousness.
