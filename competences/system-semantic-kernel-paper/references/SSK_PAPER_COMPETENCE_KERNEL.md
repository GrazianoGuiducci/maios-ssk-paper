The central thesis is:

> **SSK is the continuing semantic organization through which a situated field
> can let source, intent, possibility and competence converge into a sufficient
> resultant without making the current representation the boundary of what is
> possible. The resultant is a local determination and moving zero: it preserves
> still-material causal relations, keeps unresolved possibility reachable and
> becomes the changed origin of the following field.**

This does not posit a disembodied executable kernel. Every actual incarnation
requires physical/computational carriers. The distinction identifies the
semantic relations whose identity must not be reduced to one carrier, file
layout, model, sensor stack or host mechanism.

`Semantic` does not mean `natural-language only`. Language, image, spatial
representation, code, interface state, proprioception, radar, lidar, touch,
telemetry, remote sensing, other agents and human observation can contribute
through different transformations when they make a relation causally present.
Raw availability is not yet semantic pertinence; provenance, transformation,
presence, pertinence, uncertainty, interpretation and effect authority remain
distinct.

The receiver relation is represented analytically as:

```text
K_sigma + H_i -> K_i
```

where `K_sigma` denotes the semantic-relational organization that must remain
sufficiently invariant, `H_i` the actual receiving field and `K_i` its situated
operational incarnation. `H_i` can include sources, language/visual/sensor
modalities, tools, interfaces, persistence, embodiment, constraints and effect
authority. The notation is explanatory, not a new runtime component.

The 16 September ChatGPT/Codex/owner-native competence exercise is a bounded
conceptual specimen: related semantic competence can be exercised through
different host means. It does not establish universal transferability,
equivalent performance or a robotic implementation.

## Research Object

The primary object is **System Semantic Kernel (SSK)**: the selected typology
through which the Kernel operating here is understood and distinguished from
other kernel forms. The living D-ND / KA competence field is the source system
through which this typology is observed. The Paper and the public MAIOS
identity are owner-native projections of the same object, while products,
packages, hosts and robotic/runtime incarnations retain their own proof states.

The wider phenomenon is **artificial cognitive becoming in agentic and robotic
systems**: how a system can act from a situated semantic field, observe or
infer what its action and environment produce, understand that resultant in
relation to the system in which it participates, take its own method as an
object, compose or form competences and change how later action can begin.

The research asks what this becoming can make possible for:

- operators who need systems that preserve meaning and continuity while work
  changes;
- projects and organizations that must retain know-how as situated competence,
  not only stored instructions;
- agentic systems whose capabilities and contexts evolve;
- robotic and embodied systems in which perception, action, remote sensing,
  environment, authority, safety and real-time constraints become part of the
  field;
- societies that may organize knowledge, responsibility, cooperation and
  technological participation through new human-AI relations.

These are open possibility fields. The Paper distinguishes what is source,
represented, incarnated, observed, inferred, interpreted and still open.

## Semantic Kernel Relation

A System Semantic Kernel is not merely a repository of meanings, a router, a
prompt stack, a sensor bus or a collection of skills. Its object is the
relation through which meaning, participation, operation, resultant and
continuation remain coupled inside a system.

A useful explanatory resolution remains:

```text
O_n = observation or present source relation
F_n = open situated field, including its horizon
Q_n = pertinent relation that takes form in the event
A_n = operation or manifestation
R_n = observable resultant or genuine non-collapse
I_n = situated interpretation of the causal difference
F_n+1 = following field, possibly transformed by R_n and I_n

O_n + F_n -> Q_n -> A_n -> R_n -> I_n -> F_n+1
```

Revision 0.4 clarifies that `O_n` may arise through linguistic, visual,
sensor-derived, human or other qualified sources, and that `A_n` can include
physical or distributed action. The notation remains a reading aid, not a
mechanically sequential runtime.

A complementary explanatory architecture is:

```text
reachable informational / possibility field
-> modality / carrier
-> perception or inference
-> situated presence
-> pertinence / competence
-> operation / manifestation
-> resultant
-> causal readback
-> changed reachable field
```

Several relations can be simultaneous, recursive or distributed.

## Integrated Field, Use And Obsolescence

The SSK corpus integrates every relation that still changes the research
object, its argument or the capabilities through which it can continue. This
is integration through current use, not concatenation of every historical
artifact. A predecessor, notation, implementation or explanatory form that no
longer owns a live relation becomes obsolete as current authority and cools
into causal genealogy.

The semantic possibility carried by an obsolete form is not deleted. If a
later field makes that relation pertinent, it re-enters through its source and
present effect without restoring the old form merely because it came first.

When pertinent live relations already participate through reachable
competences, reconstruction, rediscovery and repeated operator-correction
latency tend toward zero. `Zero latency` remains an operator direction and
research hypothesis about semantic reconstruction, not a claim of zero model,
network, sensing, tool or runtime latency.

Semantic decompilation resolves a situated movement into source, intent,
context, meaning, possibility, perception/inference when relevant, competence,
operation, resultant, interpretation, causal readback and reentry. Programming
these relations to appear means incarnating them through the present receiver's
actual means; it does not mean reverse engineering model weights or fixing one
sensor/runtime architecture as SSK.

## Competence

A competence is a situated faculty of the Kernel. It understands its domain
and participation in the system, acts through available means, reads back its
effective resultant and can modify how it will act afterward. When pertinent,
it can:

- reread its own operation;
- understand its participation in the larger system;
- take its own method as an object;
- compose or form other competences;
- use owner-native knowledge without copying another receiver's mechanics;
- evolve through use and later non-identical resultants.

Situated, self-reflexive, system-aware, autological, meta and autopoietic are
possible operational dimensions, not compulsory levels, scores, promotional
labels or proof of subjective consciousness.
